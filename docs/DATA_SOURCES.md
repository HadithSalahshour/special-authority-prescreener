# Data sources and attribution

## BC PharmaCare policy material

Policy source:

- [Special Authority drug list](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/health/practitioner-professional-resources/pharmacare/programs/special-authority/sa-drug-list)

The public repository does not redistribute BC policy text. Its bundled demo cache is
fictional and exists only to exercise the software. Raw downloaded pages and locally
generated real-policy caches are excluded from Git; the scraper lets users retrieve
current public pages for their own local use, subject to the Province's terms.

The Province's general [copyright notice](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/home/copyright)
states that website material may not be reproduced or redistributed without prior
permission unless a specific licence applies. Confirm permission before publishing any
downloaded policy text or derived cache.

This project is independent and does not imply Government of British Columbia or BC
PharmaCare endorsement. Government names, marks, and logos are not used as project
branding.

## Demonstration records

`data/demo_patients.json` contains programmatically generated, fictional records.
They are provided only to exercise the software. They are not de-identified clinical
records and are not suitable for medical research conclusions.
